Confidence Calibration

This shows how well your stated confidence matches real outcomes. Aim for your 70% confidence decisions to succeed ~70% of the time.

You need at least 10–15 reviewed decisions for meaningful patterns.

How to use Judgment OS

  1. Capture at decision time — Write the decision before you know the outcome. Include options, reasoning, expected result, and your confidence level.
  2. Set a review date — Usually 30, 90, or 180 days later (or when the outcome becomes clear).
  3. Review honestly — When the date arrives, record what actually happened and rate the outcome.
  4. Look for patterns — Over time the Calibration tab will show where your judgment is strong or overconfident.

Your data stays only in this browser (localStorage). Export regularly so you never lose it.
