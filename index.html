How to use it

  1. Read Today. If the action is “fill diesel”, do that before you journal it.
  2. Capture the call in Decisions with a review date.
  3. When the date arrives, write what actually happened.
  4. Use Calibration to see whether your confidence matches outcomes.

Journal data never leaves this browser unless you Export. Intelligence on the public board is a snapshot, not a live feed from every government API. Flood maps: AEGIS Floodwatch.
