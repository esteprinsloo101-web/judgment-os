4. Privacy Notice (POPIA)

Last updated: 7 September 2026

4.1 What we collect

Depending on how you use Judgment OS, we may process:

  • payment and identity-related data processed by Paystack (we do not store full card numbers);
  • email / name you provide for support or checkout;
  • technical logs (IP, browser type, pages) from hosting;
  • optional content you type into the desk (budget numbers, notes) — often stored only on your device/browser unless a feature says otherwise.

4.2 Why we process it

To provide the service, take payment, prevent fraud, support you, improve the product, and meet legal duties.

4.3 Sharing

We use processors such as Paystack (payments) and website hosting (e.g. GitHub Pages). They process data under their own terms. We do not sell your personal information.

4.4 Your rights (POPIA)

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information we hold, or object to certain processing, subject to law. Contact the email address on your Paystack receipt. You may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa).

4.5 Security and retention

We use reasonable technical measures. No method is perfectly secure. We keep records as needed for tax, dispute, and legal periods, then delete or anonymise when no longer needed.

4.6 Children

Judgment OS paid plans are for adults (18+). We do not knowingly target children.

4.7 International

Hosts or processors may store data outside South Africa. Where that happens, we rely on appropriate protections required by law.
