2. Refund Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

2.1 Digital product

Judgment OS is a digital service / digital content. Access to paid features is generally provided immediately (or within a short time) after successful Paystack payment.

2.2 When refunds apply

We will refund (or instruct Paystack to refund) when:

  • you were charged in error (duplicate charge, wrong amount);
  • paid features were not delivered due to our fault and we cannot restore access within a reasonable time after you contact us;
  • required by the Consumer Protection Act, the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act, or other applicable law;
  • we cancel your plan and choose to refund unused prepaid time as a goodwill or legal step.

2.3 When refunds usually do not apply

Subject to your non-waivable legal rights:

  • change of mind after access to paid digital features has been provided;
  • dissatisfaction with market data, prompts, or “calls” (these are informational, not advice);
  • failure to cancel before the next billing date (see Cancellation);
  • chargebacks started without contacting us first where the product worked as described.

2.4 Cooling-off (ECT Act)

Where section 44 of the ECT Act gives you a cooling-off right, that right may not apply once performance of the digital service has begun or digital content has been supplied with your consent — as is typical for instant-access desks. If you believe a statutory cooling-off still applies to your order, email the email address on your Paystack receipt within the statutory period with your Paystack reference and we will assess in good faith.

2.5 How to request a refund

Email the email address on your Paystack receipt with: Paystack payment reference, plan name, date, and reason. We aim to respond within 5 business days. Approved refunds are processed via Paystack to your original payment method; bank timing varies.

2.6 Chargebacks

Please contact us before lodging a chargeback. Unfounded chargebacks may lead to suspension of access.
