3. Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

3.1 How subscriptions work

Paid plans may be one-time or recurring (as shown at Paystack checkout). Recurring plans renew until cancelled.

3.2 How to cancel

Cancel anytime by:

1. using Paystack’s customer/subscription cancel link in your payment receipt email (if provided), or

2. emailing the email address on your Paystack receipt with your Paystack reference and asking to cancel future billing.

We will confirm cancellation of future charges. Cancellation does not automatically refund the current paid period unless the Refund Policy applies.

3.3 Effect of cancellation

  • No further renewals after cancellation is processed.
  • You keep access until the end of the period already paid for (unless we terminate for breach or law requires otherwise).
  • After that period, paid features stop; free/public parts of the site may remain available.

3.4 Price changes

If we change plan prices, existing subscribers will be notified where practical before the new price hits a renewal. You may cancel before the renewal if you do not accept the new price.

3.5 Our right to suspend

We may suspend or cancel access for non-payment, fraud, abuse, or legal risk. Where required by law, we will refund unused prepaid amounts.
